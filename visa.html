Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents are needed for a visa?

Usually a passport, photographs, travel and stay details and financial proof. The exact list depends on the country and visa type, and we share a checklist for yours.

How long does visa processing take?

It depends on the country, the season and the visa type. The time shown for each visa is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Is visa approval guaranteed?

No. The embassy or immigration authority decides every application. We make sure your documents and form are in order.

Can you help with flight and hotel bookings for the application?

We can advise on the bookings usually required and arrange them with you.

My country is not listed. What should I do?

Use the custom visa request form and we will check the requirements for you.
