Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a hotel through Dharti Holidays?

Pick a destination, add the hotels you like to your trip list and send the enquiry. We confirm availability and the final price, then complete the booking for you.

Are the prices shown final?

No. They are indicative starting prices per night. The final rate depends on dates, room type and availability, and we confirm it before you pay.

Can you book a hotel that is not listed?

Yes. Tell us the hotel or the area and your dates, and we will check availability and rates.

What do Budget, Deluxe and Luxury mean?

They are broad categories to help you compare: Budget for simple, value stays; Deluxe for comfortable mid-range hotels; Luxury for premium hotels and resorts.

Is breakfast included?

Meal plans vary by hotel and room type. The amenities of each hotel are listed, and we confirm the meal plan for your dates in the quote.

Can I book several hotels for a multi-city trip?

Yes. Add hotels from different destinations to the same trip list and send one enquiry for the whole itinerary.
