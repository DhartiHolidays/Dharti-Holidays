Frequently Asked Questions

What is in a destination guide?

Each guide covers where the place is, the best time to visit, an overview, places to see, activities and local food, with photos and an enquiry form.

How do I plan a trip to a destination?

Open its guide and use the enquiry form at the end. Tell us your dates and number of travellers and we will send a personalised plan and quote.

Can you arrange flights, hotels and visas for the destination?

Yes. We can arrange the whole trip — flights, hotels, transfers, sightseeing and visa assistance where needed.

Do you organise trips to places that are not listed?

Yes. If you do not see your destination, send us an enquiry and we will plan it for you.

When is the best time to travel?

It differs by destination and is mentioned in each guide. We can also suggest dates based on the weather, crowds and your budget.
