Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a package?

Inclusions vary by package and are confirmed in your quote. If you also need flights, visas or extra sightseeing, we can add them.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. We adjust the number of days, hotel category, sightseeing and add-ons for your budget and dates.

How do I book a package?

Send an enquiry from the package card, by WhatsApp or by phone. We share the itinerary and price, and confirm the booking after you approve it.

Are the prices shown final?

The prices on the cards are starting prices, per person or per couple as stated, and change with season, hotel category and group size.

Do you offer honeymoon and group packages?

Yes. Use the Honeymoon and Corporate & Group tabs, or tell us what you have in mind.

When is the best time to travel?

It depends on the destination. Our destination guides mention the best months, and we can suggest dates based on your plans.
